Help Center

Answers to the usual questions.

How do I upgrade my plan?

Click + Upgrade plan in the sidebar capacity card, pick Pro 100 GB, pay — done in a minute.

Why is an order stuck on Pending?

No courier accepted it yet. Open Couriers, toggle more riders online, or enable Auto-assign in Settings.

How do I add a new courier?

Couriers → invite by email. They appear here once they accept and go online.
